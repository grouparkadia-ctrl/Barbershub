Common questions before you visit

Open questions and answers+
Am I employed by BARBERS HUB?

No. BARBERS HUB is a workspace for independent professionals. You manage your clients, schedule, services, prices and income.

Do I need a full client list?

No. You can begin with a flexible plan while building your client base. You should still have a realistic plan for attracting and serving your own clients.

Does BARBERS HUB provide clients or walk-ins?

We do not guarantee bookings or walk-ins. A walk-in goes to the first barber who wants and is available to take the client. Optional marketing and client-acquisition support can be discussed during the tour.

What is the difference between a tour and a reservation?

A tour is a free visit to see the workspace and ask questions. A reservation is paid working time with a prepared barber station.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. You can reserve flexible working time without a long-term contract. FLEX subscriptions are available separately for regular cooperation.

Can I keep my own brand?

Yes. Your professional identity, client relationships and brand remain yours.

Can I set my own prices and keep my income?

Yes. You operate independently, set your services and prices, accept client payments yourself and keep your income.

Is business support included?

The workspace is provided according to your plan. Additional accounting, marketing, content and business guidance are optional and discussed separately.

What do I need to bring?

Bring your professional tools, the products you work with and your own booked clients. BARBERS HUB provides sanitary consumables such as neck strips, paper towels, towels and disinfectants.

What legal status do I need?

To start working, you need a legally registered form of self-employment or a company. We can discuss your situation during the tour; consult VID or an accountant for tax and legal advice.

Who is BARBERS HUB not suitable for?

It may not suit someone who expects the workspace to manage their clients, schedule and professional responsibilities. We provide the environment and access to support; you remain responsible for your work and business.

How is the price calculated?

The price is 0.10 € for each reserved working minute. The minimum reservation is 60 minutes (6 €), and the daily maximum is 60 €.

Is preparation and clean-up time charged?

No. We add 15 minutes before and 15 minutes after your working time free of charge.

How far ahead can I reserve?

You can reserve an available time up to 20 days ahead.

What happens after payment?

You receive a booking confirmation and receipt. The confirmed time is reserved in the shared calendar.

Are FLEX subscriptions available?

Yes. FLEX subscriptions for 10, 15 or 20 days are available for long-term cooperation. Contact us to discuss the most suitable option.
